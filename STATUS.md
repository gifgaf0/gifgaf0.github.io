# Status — October 8, 2026

**In one paragraph.** This program explored whether the vacuum could be a physical medium: a crystal that is also a superfluid (a *supersolid*), with light as its shear waves and particles as knotted vortices in it. **The author closed it on October 7 (ledger V4.97, §2.97).** The October audit tested every place where the picture could break, and it broke at each one:
- gravitational waves have the wrong polarization;
- moving matter would lose energy to a sound slower than light;
- the knots are not protected from untying;
- the proton-mass match was the proton mass run backwards;
- to let light through, the lattice cells would have to be finer than the Planck length.

**What carries forward:**
- the mathematics, at its registers;
- three standalone results: the second-sound paper (a negative result about supersolid vacua in general), the negative result on the program's lattice cryptosystem, and the alpha-decay data with its break at radium;
- the idea that ran through the program, as a conjecture only: matter is energy held in place by geometry, and a structure lasts when it stores energy efficiently enough.

No successor program is opened; the ledger records the conditions one would have to meet (§2.97.D). Corrections to the public work are drafted for the author. Nothing is published, deposited or sent.

## How it closed (October 6–8)

- **The central idea failed first.** In a supersolid medium, matter and light cannot share one speed limit unless two unrelated numbers are tuned to agree to about one part in 10²³. That is written up as the standalone second-sound paper.
- **The audit follow-up then removed the remaining physics claims:**
  - gravitational waves have no carrier in the medium;
  - the proton-mass "prediction" was the proton mass run backwards, and its one real test fails;
  - nothing protects baryon number;
  - neither static picture of gravity holds up.
- **It corrected the public work, as drafts for the author's approval:**
  - the gauge paper's hypercharge sign rule and its Cabibbo value both fail;
  - "zero free parameters" becomes a fit with its inputs named, and the W-mass and weak-angle formulas are retired;
  - the alpha-decay paper's second break does not survive a comparison at fixed neutron number.
- **It banked the cryptography result and cleaned up the ledger:**
  - the program's lattice cryptosystem fails at its own parameters, and a short negative-result note is drafted;
  - the "tired light" redshift idea fails against supernova time dilation;
  - the electron's 2π closure, and the mass exponent built on it, rest on an unmade choice of vacuum;
  - the neutron-moment "factor of 4" was the wrong quartet;
  - fourteen mathematical slips are corrected.
- **It asked how fine the vacuum's lattice would have to be for light to get through.** The highest-energy photons on record cap the grains at about the Planck length. At the author's floor of 50 cells per grain, the cells must be finer than 0.026 Planck lengths.
- **The author then closed the program (V4.97).** Every open task in the ledger was sorted:

## Established

- **Mathematics.**
  - **PSL(2,7) spectral rigidity.** The results are correct and public on Zenodo, but not new: the structure is a known one (a "Gelfand pair"). Three journals declined the paper, so there will be no further submission. A version 3 is drafted: it cites the classical literature and corrects one count (146 group elements fix circles, not 104).
  - **Gauge-group paper.** The Császár-polyhedron paper is published on Zenodo. A correction (v6.4) is drafted, because its hypercharge sign rule and its Cabibbo value fail (next section). What remains new is matching the three fermion modes to the polyhedron's three Hamiltonian cycles.
  - **The polyhedron's symmetry.** The Császár polyhedron's faces have a symmetry group of order 42 (AGL(1,7)). PSL(2,7), of order 168, is the symmetry group of each of its two Fano planes, not of the polyhedron. Several ledger entries had assumed otherwise, and the gauge paper never claimed it; those entries are now annotated.
  - **Sedenion zero divisors.** Moreno's 1998 criterion proves the even-term rule for clean zero divisors, and all 7.2 million clean elements were checked exactly. Several smaller facts were corrected:
    - every Fano symmetry lifts to an octonion symmetry once signs are allowed;
    - K₈ has six 1-factorizations, not two;
    - a stabilizer the ledger called V₄ × ℤ₂ is the dihedral group D₄;
    - the structures known in the literature as de Marrais's "box-kites" are now credited where they appear.
  - **Ropelength paper.** Its twist-knot family reproduces published knot minimizations to 0.26 %.
  - **Tight Borromean rings.** The tightest known Borromean rings need 58.006 units of rope per unit of tube radius. The four-arc version the ledger uses needs 48·arctan √7 = 58.053, computed twice.
- **The model medium exists and behaves as claimed.**
  - A Bose condensate with a soft-core interaction crystallizes into a hexagonal supersolid in two dimensions, and into a close-packed (hcp-like) stack in three.
  - It carries a genuine propagating shear wave (the "light" candidate) and two compression sounds, all computed independently twice and checked against controls.
- **The medium's hidden internal waves.**
  - The field has sixteen components, and rotating into the unused ones costs no energy. That gives seven extra wave branches.
  - Those branches are very slow, with a speed limit of zero: anything that couples to them loses energy however slowly it moves.
  - Knots built from twisted internal directions do couple to them. The knots' declared density coupling does not.
- **Light-as-shear is consistent on its own terms.** In a randomly oriented polycrystal the two shear polarizations travel at one speed; this is an exact symmetry result. Weak alignment splits them only at second order, and all the coefficients are computed.
- **Vortex protection.**
  - Ordinary vortex windings can be protected, but only if a particular symmetry-breaking term is chosen. The geometry allows that term but does not force it, so it is an input, not a derivation.
  - The "half-quantum" vortices are protected only by accident: the action's true symmetry allows a term that removes that protection.
  - The electron's "2π closure" therefore holds only if that input is made (new). The same applies to the 2π/Φ mass exponent and to two "stable quantum" verdicts that rest on it. The vacuum of record protects no winding at all. No number changes, because the mass scale was fitted anyway.

## Dead, and why

- **The original "physics engine"** (particle masses from an E8 → H4 reduction). Its glueball predictions are contradicted by lattice QCD, and H4 is incompatible with the symmetry of the strong force.
- **"Each crystal layer filters long waves by a fixed factor."** The crystal turns out to be transparent to its own long-wavelength density waves, as superfluids generally are.
- **Gravity carried by the medium's compression sound.**
  - A knot moving faster than that sound radiates into it, like a supersonic boat making a wake.
  - Cosmic rays cross the galaxy at nearly light speed without losing energy. At any microscopic grain size, the predicted loss is about 40 orders of magnitude too fast.
- **A shared speed limit for matter and light in a supersolid vacuum.**
  - A supersolid has a second, slower compression sound (*second sound*).
  - The knots' cores have a density hole that cannot be filled away, so the knots couple to it.
  - In the model, second sound runs at 6–80 % of the light (shear) speed and is never faster, so fast matter would shed sound. The result is still about 39 orders too fast.
- **Gravitational waves in the same channel as light** (new, October 6–7).
  - The medium's candidate gravitational wave would show up in a detector as a *vector* wave (spin 1), not a tensor wave (spin 2).
  - LIGO–Virgo's polarization test on GW170817 prefers tensor over vector by a factor of about 10²¹.
  - The ledger's "GW170817 pass" is withdrawn, and the medium has no carrier for gravitational waves.
- **The proton-mass prediction** (new).
  - The predicted "rope length" of 60.194 turns out to be the proton mass fed backwards through the mass formula.
  - The real tightest Borromean rings are at most 58.006. Feeding that into the formula gives a proton of 759 MeV, 19 % light.
  - By the ledger's own rule the entry goes back to conjecture.
- **Baryon number as unbreakable linking** (new).
  - Vortex loops in superfluids cut through each other and come apart.
  - Protecting them needs vortex charges that do not commute, and the medium has none.
  - No energy barrier has been computed.
- **The two static pictures of gravity** (new).
  - The "bowl" picture said small objects do not feel a large, locally flat bowl. But flat means no curvature, not no slope: neutrons, atoms and antihydrogen are all measured falling in Earth's field.
  - The "tension" picture said defects in a tensioned lattice attract as 1/r². In an elastic medium, isolated defects of this kind do not attract at all (Eshelby, 1956).
  - Both pictures also fail the equivalence principle, which is now a standing test (next section).
- **The W-mass and weak-angle formulas** (new). m_W = m₀φ²⁷ gives 82.13 GeV against a measured 80.37 GeV, 132 standard deviations away. sin²θ_W = φ⁻³ never said at which energy, or in which scheme, it should hold. Both are retired.
- **The Cabibbo angle as 3/13** (new). The gauge paper states it as sin θ_C, which the data exclude by 7.6–8.5 standard deviations. Read as tan θ_C it fits, but that reading was chosen after seeing the data.
- **The gauge paper's hypercharge sign rule** (new). Its charges do not add up across the three modes, and with either color convention one quark sector gets a charge no real quark of that color has. Furey's own charges stand.
- **The lattice cryptosystem ("SLWE")** (new, October 7).
  - Its structured public matrix collapses to rank 76 at every module rank from 7 up, whatever the seeds, because it factors through one fixed 256 × 112 matrix of rank 76.
  - Even with a fully random matrix, the specified parameters give about 2^51 security by the standard lattice estimator, not 2^128: the modulus is far too large for the noise.
  - Decryption never fails (the noise is at most 258 against a threshold of about 10⁹). The earlier failure-rate figure came from a Gaussian tail outside the noise's range.
  - A short negative-result note for the IACR ePrint archive is drafted and awaits the author.
- **The ζ-tax ("tired light") redshift** (new). It made redshift a small amplitude loss at every lattice corner. That reddens light without stretching time. Distant supernovae are time-stretched by exactly the cosmological factor (DES 2024: exponent 1.003 ± 0.011, where tired light gives 0).
- **"The factor of 4 is the spin-3/2 quartet"** (new). The 4 in the quark-model neutron moment is a spin-½ coupling weight. The spin-3/2 states are the Δ resonances, whose neutral member has zero moment in the same model. The Gate 2a work since June keeps its mathematics, but not this reading.
- **A second alpha-decay break at uranium** (new). Compared at the same neutron number, the step at Z = 92 changes sign and is smaller than the drift with neutron number. The break at radium (Z = 88) holds at every neutron number and has a standard explanation (octupole deformation).
- **Earlier retractions on the record:** an octonion-structure hypothesis, a "harmonic saturation" mass rule, and a meson-nonet interpretation.
