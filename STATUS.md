# Status — October 4, 2026

**In one paragraph.** This program explores whether the vacuum could be a physical medium: a crystal that is also a superfluid (a *supersolid*), with light as its shear waves and particles as knotted vortices in it. The pure mathematics is in good shape and public. On the physics side the medium has been built and measured in detail, but the central physical idea has now failed a decisive test: in this kind of medium, matter and light cannot share one speed limit unless two unrelated numbers are tuned to agree to about one part in 10²³. That failure is being written up as a standalone negative result.

## Established

- **Mathematics.**
  - The spectral-rigidity results for the group PSL(2,7) are correct and public on Zenodo, but not new. The structure is a known one (a "Gelfand pair"). Three journals declined it, so there will be no further submission.
  - The Császár-polyhedron gauge-group paper is published on Zenodo.
  - The ropelength paper's twist-knot family reproduces published knot minimizations to 0.26 %.
- **The model medium exists and behaves as claimed.** A Bose condensate with a soft-core interaction crystallizes into a hexagonal supersolid in two dimensions and into a close-packed (hcp-like) stack in three. It carries a genuine propagating shear wave (the "light" candidate) and two compression sounds, all computed independently twice and checked against controls.
- **Light-as-shear is consistent on its own terms.** In a randomly oriented polycrystal the two shear polarizations travel at one speed (an exact symmetry result); weak alignment splits them only at second order, with all the coefficients computed.
- **Vortex matter can be topologically protected**, but only if a particular symmetry-breaking term is chosen. The geometry allows it but does not force it, so it is an input, not a derivation.

## Dead, and why

- **The original "physics engine"** (particle masses from an E8 → H4 reduction): its glueball predictions are contradicted by lattice QCD, and H4 is incompatible with the symmetry of the strong force.
- **"Each crystal layer filters long waves by a fixed factor"**: the crystal turns out to be transparent to its own long-wavelength density waves, as superfluids generally are.
- **Gravity carried by the medium's compression sound**: a knot moving faster than that sound radiates it, like a supersonic boat making a wake. Cosmic rays travel across the galaxy at nearly light speed without losing energy, and at any microscopic grain size the predicted loss is about 40 orders of magnitude too fast. No microscopic grain size passes.
- **A shared speed limit for matter and light in a supersolid vacuum.** A supersolid has a second, slower compression sound (*second sound*). The knots' cores have a density hole that cannot be filled away without the knot spreading out, so they couple to it. Measured in the model, second sound runs at 6–80 % of the light (shear) speed and is never faster: fast matter would shed sound. Re-checking the cosmic-ray test on the measured slow sound recovers only about three orders of magnitude, and the result is still about 39 orders too fast. Nothing in the physics ties the two speeds together.
- Several earlier readings are retracted on the record: an octonion-structure hypothesis, a "harmonic saturation" mass rule, and a meson-nonet interpretation.

## Open

- **What would rescue a medium vacuum?** It needs a medium in which matter is not a defect dragging through a slower sound. The leading candidates are vacua with no preferred frame, whose pressure equals minus their energy density, and particles as relativistic knotted solitons (Skyrme and Faddeev–Niemi type), which carry their own light cone automatically. This exploration has started. First result: the standard two-field topology does not protect a three-ring ("Borromean") baryon, so its stability would need a different kind of vacuum or an energy barrier.
- **Light and gravitational waves in one channel**: partly settled (the speed equality is a symmetry identity in the untextured case). It is not settled for an aligned vacuum.
- **Physical scale**: the medium has no grain size consistent with the cosmic-ray bound.
- **Masses**: the proton mass is not yet a prediction (it uses a fitted anchor). Several mass and nuclear-decay patterns stay at the conjecture level, with stated tests.
- **Papers in motion**: a short negative-result paper on the shared-speed-limit failure. The draft is ready; an independent recomputation of its numbers is queued.
