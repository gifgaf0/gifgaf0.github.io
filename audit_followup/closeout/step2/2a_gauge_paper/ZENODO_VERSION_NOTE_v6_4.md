> Version 6.4 (October 2026) corrects the paper and adds no new claim. The alternating sign rule for hypercharge is withdrawn: it does not give the three modes consistent charges, and the charges are those of Furey's number operator, Q = N/3. The Cabibbo value is downgraded. As stated, sin θ_C = 3/13 is excluded by the 2024 data (7.6–8.5 standard deviations). The number does agree with tan θ_C, but that reading was chosen after seeing the data, so it is labelled a post-hoc observation, not a result. Three further statements are fixed. First, the automorphism group of the quaternions is SO(3), not SU(2): the unit quaternions, which form SU(2), act on them by conjugation with kernel ±1. Second, a torus embedded in space inherits an even spin structure, antiperiodic around two of its three nonzero cycle classes and periodic around one; the all-periodic structure is the odd one, and no embedding induces it. Third, the uniqueness of the seven-vertex torus is classical (Möbius, 1886), and Bokowski and Eggert (1991) classified its realizations. The description of the 14 faces is also corrected, and the title now names only what the paper still claims. A last set of smaller corrections changes no conclusion; for example, 3/13 is now called a combinatorial invariant of the triangulation rather than a topological one.
