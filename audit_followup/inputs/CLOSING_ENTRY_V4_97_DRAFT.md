### §2.97 — Closing the Substrate Program (V4.97)

*(Folded V4.97, October [DATE], 2026, on the author's decision of October 7, 2026 to close the substrate program and carry its organizing idea forward. This is a decision record, not a computation: nothing is newly derived, so under the proportionality rule there is no pre-registration and no second leg. Every verdict cited is the one already on record in the section named. Affected rows carry [→ V4.97 (§2.97)] pointers. No §3.x: nothing is retracted here that was not already retracted.)*

**In plain language.** This program tried to build the physical world out of one material: a supersolid vacuum whose shear wave is light and whose knots are matter. The October audit tested every place where that picture could break, and it broke at each one:
- its gravitational waves have the wrong polarization;
- moving matter would lose energy to waves slower than light;
- its knots are not protected from untying;
- its proton-mass match was the proton mass run backwards;
- to keep light from scattering, its lattice cells would have to be finer than the Planck length.

What survives is the mathematics, a few standalone results, and the idea that runs through the whole program: matter is energy held in place by geometry, and a structure exists when it stores energy efficiently enough to last. That idea never depended on the material, and none of the reasons the material failed count against it. This entry closes the material, carries the idea forward as a conjecture, and states the conditions any new attempt must meet.

**A. What closes, and why.** The *substrate program* closes: the vacuum as a Gross–Pitaevskii supersolid (single- and 16-component), light as its transverse shear wave, matter as knots and textures in it, and gravity as a property of the medium. Each pillar already has a recorded verdict.
1. **Gravity.**
   - The longitudinal acoustic bridge retired when KC3 fired (§2.91.H, V4.67).
   - Both static pictures were downgraded, and KC-EP fires for both (§2.89, §2.90; §2.92.D).
   - The medium has no carrier for gravitational waves. Its transverse channel is pure vector, and GW170817 prefers tensor over vector at log₁₀ B = 20.81 (§2.92.A).
2. **Matter in motion.**
   - A density source drives the subluminal second-sound branch generically, and c₂/c_T never reaches 1 (at most 0.79, on the metastable branch). At ξ = ℓ_P the loss length falls short by about 39 orders (§2.91.V, V4.89; the paper-cited numbers closed two-leg at V4.90).
   - Texture-type matter also radiates into the gapless, quadratic internal branches at every speed (§2.92.B).
3. **Matter at rest.**
   - §2.15 is retracted to Conjecture. The ideal Borromean ropelength is at most 58.006, which gives m_p = 758.7 MeV; 60.194 and 80.95 are m_p inverted.
   - Linking is unprotected.
   - §2.1 is a fit with dof −1 (§2.92.C).
   - The electron's 2π closure is conditional on the unmade import I6 (§2.94.C2 P3), and the μ_n "factor of 4" reading is downgraded (§2.94.C2 P4).
4. **Light.**
   - c_T ≡ c survives only as the units election (ANNEX-CDEF-1).
   - Its polycrystal medium needs grains of d ≤ 2.08×10⁻³⁵ m (1.28 ℓ_P) under LHAASO's PeV photons (§2.95). At the elected floor of N = 50 cells per grain, the lattice spacing must be a ≤ 4.15×10⁻³⁷ m (0.026 ℓ_P) (§2.96).
   - The declared 12–47 ℓ_P lattice fits no cell.
5. **Redshift.** ζ-tax gate 3 closed, failed as framed: the mechanism is tired light, while DES measures b = 1.003 ± 0.005 (stat) ± 0.010 (sys) (§2.94.C2 P1).

**Why close rather than continue.** Every remaining physical item rests on a declaration or a named import: the units election, I4, I6, the immiscibility import, a sub-Planck lattice. Each verdict above could be undone only by adding another import or another mechanism, never by deriving it from what is on record. This completes, for the substrate, the pattern M.BRIDGE records: the mathematics survives audit, and every bridge to a dimensionful observable needs an import it cannot derive.

The structural reason is the one the second-sound paper names. A medium has a rest frame, so matter and light share one causal cone only by tuning: |1 − c₂/c_T| ≲ 10⁻²³ for the highest-energy cosmic rays. In a Lorentz-invariant field theory a moving soliton is the boost of a static one, so the cone is shared automatically.

3. **The organizing idea, at R3.** Recorded in the author's words (October 7, 2026):
   > "Energy, entropy, or whatever is the same thing scaled up and held together by geometric chance or preference (in the valence electron sense). Scaling up is not guaranteed, it is efficient energy storage, or efficient enough to warrant existing."
   >
   > "They are all the same thing at different geometric configurations, holding different energy level[s]."

   *Standard-physics reading (R3, for orientation only; not a result).* Each point below is established physics:
   - Rest mass is energy held in place. E = mc² already counts binding, and binding lowers mass.
   - A structure persists where binding wins the free-energy balance, F = E − TS.
   - Discreteness comes from being bound.
   - Attributes that come in whole steps (charge, spin) need discrete configurations, which is what topology supplies.

   The idea is consistent with established physics at every scale. It is not yet a claim: it becomes one only by meeting the conditions in D.
