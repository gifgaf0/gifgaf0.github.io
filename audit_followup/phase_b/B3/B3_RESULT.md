# B3 — Paper VII §9.1 and the calculators: scoped wording, electroweak formulas retired (DRAFTS)

**Plain-language summary.** Paper VII and the calculators said the framework has "zero free parameters." It does not. The masses are fitted at leading order from:
- one measured anchor, the electron mass;
- one chosen length scale, ξ_vac = 100φ;
- a per-particle choice of knot, A, Z_f and length L.

That is about as many choices as there are masses to match. Four drafts now say so: the public site calculator, both project-store calculators, and Paper VII. Each draft names those inputs.

The two electroweak formulas are retired as predictions:
- m_W = m₀φ²⁷ gives 82.13 GeV against a measured 80.37 GeV, 2.2% high and 132 standard deviations away.
- sin²θ_W = φ⁻³ never stated the energy scale or scheme it applies to.

The drafts also carry Phase A's proton result: the ropelength prediction fails, and the proton "match" is the proton mass run backwards.

Two further errors turned up and are corrected in the same drafts:
- **A false group statement.** The texts say the six-element group F₇* sits inside PSL(2,7). It cannot: PSL(2,7) has no element of order 6. The subgroup that fixes three non-collinear Fano points is S₃, which also has order 6.
- **Mixed length units.** The quark lengths are ropelengths measured per tube *diameter*. The electron and proton lengths are per tube *radius*. This closes a lead left open in Phase A.

Nothing is published. The live site (`index.html` on `main`) and the project-store files are unchanged.

## What was checked

**Numbers** (`b3_numbers.py`; PDG 2024: S. Navas et al., Phys. Rev. D 110, 030001, the electroweak review's Table 10.2 and the light-quark listings, read on the KEK mirror):

| Quantity | Formula value | PDG 2024 | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| m_W = m₀φ²⁷ | 82.1275 GeV | 80.3692 ± 0.0133 GeV | +2.19%, 132σ (147σ against the 80.360 ± 0.012 average without CDF) |
| m_Z = m_W/cos θ_W, sin²θ_W = φ⁻³ | 93.9640 GeV | 91.1880 ± 0.0020 GeV | +3.04%, 1388σ |
| sin²θ_W = φ⁻³ = 0.23607 | — | M̄S at M_Z 0.23129(4); on-shell 0.22348(10); effective leptonic 0.23161(4); M̄S at Q→0 0.23873(5) | +2.07%, +5.63%, +1.92%, −1.12% |

The mass-table rows reproduce Phase A's figures: u −5.6%, d −2.36%, s −2.75%, c −2.15%, b −0.49%, t −0.80%, τ −1.38%, μ −7.45%. The electron row is the anchor; the formula returns 0.4925 MeV at L = 2π, which is −3.6%, the r_eff(2π) slack.

**F₇* and PSL(2,7)** (`b3_checks.py`, explicit enumeration):

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Element orders of PSL(2,7) (168 elements) | 1 (×1), 2 (×21), 3 (×56), 4 (×42), 7 (×48); **no element of order 6** |
| F₇* | The diagonal torus of SL(2,7), cyclic of order 6, generated by diag(3, 5); its image in PSL(2,7) has order 3 |
| Stabilizer of a non-collinear triple ("Fano triangle") in GL(3,2) ≅ PSL(2,7) | Order 6, non-abelian, realizes all 6 permutations of the three points: **S₃**. 28 triangles, regular action on ordered frames. |

So "F₇* = {1,…,6} ⊂ PSL(2,7)" is false. This is Paper VII §3.4 and the v1.9 Baryon tab; the site's Conjecture 1 sketch says "canonical in PSL(2,7) context". The number 6 survives as |S₃|. Conjecture 1 stands as a conjecture; only its stated motivation changes. §2.70's homomorphism to an abstract ℤ/6 is unaffected.

**The L column**, closing the Phase A lead (§2.92.C(6): "the calculator's quark L values look like ropelengths per tube diameter, while L_e and L_B are per radius"):

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| The ideal trefoil is 32.7429345 per tube radius, so 16.3715 per diameter. The calculators' up-quark L is 16.372. | Przybył & Pierański, arXiv:1402.5760; also arXiv:2108.01857 and arXiv:1110.3262 (31.32 to 32.74317 per radius) |
| Every nontrivial knot has ropelength ≥ 15.66 per diameter, i.e. ≥ 31.32 per radius | Denne, Diao & Sullivan, Geom. Topol. 10 (2006) 1–26 |
| L_e = 2π is the unknot per radius; the Borromean 58.006 is per radius (Paper VII E.1.1 defines ℒ = L/R) | CFKSW, Geom. Topol. 10 (2006) 2055 |

So the u, d, s and c lengths (16.372, 21.04, 29.13, 23.60) cannot be per-radius ropelengths of any nontrivial knot. The trefoil value settles the convention for the up row. The other quark lengths sit on the same per-diameter scale but were not individually checked against tables. Read per radius, the up quark would come out at 15.6 MeV instead of 2.04. The table depends on the mixed convention; that is one more selection, and it does not change Phase A's verdict that the table is a fit.

**Why one leg.** No new number decides a verdict here:
- The retirement rests on Phase A's two-leg figures and on PDG 2024.
- The F₇* item corrects a false sentence; no register status changes.
- The L item closes a lead.

All three are annotations under the brief's rule. The choice between retiring and scoping the electroweak formulas was the brief's to offer. The drafts retire them for two reasons. Phase A counts the exponent 27 and the angle φ⁻³ as selected inputs. And scoping would need a stated scale and scheme for φ⁻³ and a derivation of 27, neither of which exists.

**The scoped wording.** The V4.46 queue (Part VI row, §2.64.A Flags 4 and 6) asked for the "scoped two-anchor" statement: m_e as the mass scale and ξ_vac as the running scale. It also asked that couplings be left out of the claim. Phase A has since shown that the table carries per-row selections too (dof −1). The drafts therefore name all three:
- one anchor (m_e);
- one selected scale (ξ_vac = 100φ), the two anchors of V4.46;
- per-particle selections (knot, A, Z_f, L).

They call the result a leading-order fit. No draft extends the claim to couplings. For α⁻¹, Paper VII §7.4 now names the selected 8π and the conjectured 84 rather than claiming zero free parameters.

**Provenance of the store sources.** The two store calculators and Paper VII were taken verbatim from the project-store reads of October 7, so the drafts start from the files of record, not from transcriptions. Their md5s are in the table. Copies are in `store/source/` and `paper_vii/source/`.

## Left for the author

- **Adoption.** Deploying `index_v3_1_DRAFT.html` as `index.html`, replacing the store calculators and adopting the Paper VII draft are the author's actions. The repo is public, so these drafts are visible on the branch now.
- **Retire or scope.** The drafts retire both electroweak formulas. Scoping would need a scale and scheme for φ⁻³ and a derivation of 27.
- **Not changed, but affected:**
  - *Paper VII §10 and the v1.9 Cosmic Echoes tab* describe gravitational waves crossing a conformal boundary, attenuated by κ/4. Phase A (A1) found that the substrate has no carrier for gravitational waves (§2.92.A). §10 needs the author's review.
  - *The name "Theorem 1"* for the mass operator. The operator is a fitting function under the §2.92 count, so the label may need revisiting.
  - *The neutrino panel and Paper VII §8* use ξ_vac³, and ξ_vac is a selected scale.
  - *The abstract's κ list* includes "electroweak mixing", while Chapter VI's κ table lists the fine-structure correction instead. This inconsistency predates the audit.
- **Conjecture 1.** With F₇* unavailable inside PSL(2,7), the author may restate Z_f = 6 as |Stab(Fano triangle)| = |S₃|, or leave it as |F₇*| with F₇* acting elsewhere, for example through AGL(1,7) on the Császár polyhedron. The drafts only remove the false inclusion.
