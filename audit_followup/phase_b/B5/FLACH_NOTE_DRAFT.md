**Subject:** The η-invariant question — an update

Dear Professor Flach,

Thank you for considering the η-invariant question I raised with you. I am writing with an update, so that you need not spend further time on it.

The reason for the question has gone away. I wanted the η-invariant in order to decide whether the internal geometry of my model fixes a Finkelstein–Rubinstein spin sign. A separate calculation has since settled this. In the model's collective-coordinate orbit, the loop traced by a 2π rotation is contractible, so the internal geometry supplies no spin sign either way.

The question as I meant it concerns the seven-dimensional orbifold S⁷/PSL(2,7), the quotient of the round seven-sphere by a linear action of PSL(2,7). If my message instead read as a question about the two-dimensional orbifold ℙ¹(2,3,7), three remarks apply:
- on a two-dimensional orbifold the Dirac η-invariant vanishes, by chirality symmetry;
- the cone point of order 2 may in any case obstruct a spin structure;
- the meaningful version of that question lives on the three-manifold Σ(2,3,7).

Should the number for S⁷/PSL(2,7) still interest you, Donnelly's formula reduces it to a finite sum over the conjugacy classes of PSL(2,7). I can compute it myself and would be glad to send you the result.

With thanks and best regards,

Matt Gifford
