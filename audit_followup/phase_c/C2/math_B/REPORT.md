**Computation** (`e_k8_1factorizations.py`)
- List the 105 perfect matchings, then backtrack to enumerate every 1-factorization exactly once.
- Group them into isomorphism classes under S₈ with union-find, using the 7 adjacent transpositions.
- Compute |Aut| two ways: as 8!/(class size), and by directly counting the permutations that fix a representative of each class.
- Count, for each class, the factor pairs whose union is two 4-cycles.

**Result.** There are 6240 labelled 1-factorizations in **6** classes. The class sizes sum to 6240.

**Ledger text**
- **L1045 (§2.85 Part B):** "The constituent result μ_p = (4μ_u − μ_d)/3 (ratio −3/2, parameter-free, ~3% to data) puts the sign and magnitude of μ_n entirely in the **factor of 4** = the spin-3/2 quartet being a single SU(2) irrep."

  - L1080: "μ_n's factor of 4 (§2.85), the two genuine 4-dim spin reps … all turn out to be **one structure** — Spin(6) = SU(4)."
- **Also carrying the identification:** L1084 ("the factor of 4 / spin-3/2 is available and μ_n → −3/2") and L1077 ("the factor of 4 is the Cℓ(6) half-spinor …"). L1077's own statement that σ₄ restricted to 2O is spin-3/2 is correct; see (c).

**Computation** (`m_magnetic_moments.py`, exact sympy)
- **Space:** three quarks, each with flavour u/d and spin up/down, give 64 dimensions. Colour is antisymmetric and suppressed.
- **States, defined only by constraints:**
  - All states are fully symmetric under swapping quarks.
  - Proton and neutron: total spin ½, spin-up, with two u quarks (proton) or one (neutron).
  - Δ states: spin 3/2, fully spin-up, with 3, 2, 1 or 0 u quarks.
  - Each defining system has exactly one solution.
  - Isospin was checked afterwards (½ for nucleons, 3/2 for Δs), not imposed.
- **Moment operator:** μ̂ = Σᵢ μ_{qᵢ} σ_z⁽ⁱ⁾.

**Result**
- **Symmetric subspace:** 20 dimensions, made of a 16-dimensional spin-3/2/isospin-3/2 part and a 4-dimensional spin-½/isospin-½ part. There are no mixed sectors.
- **Proton state:** proportional to 2|u↑u↑d↓⟩ − |u↑u↓d↑⟩ − |u↓u↑d↑⟩ plus all position permutations, with norm² 18. This is the standard SU(6) state, recovered from the constraints.

| State | μ (exact) | at μ_u = −2μ_d |
|---|---|---|
| p | (4μ_u − μ_d)/3 | −3μ_d |
| n | (4μ_d − μ_u)/3 | 2μ_d |
| Δ⁺⁺ | 3μ_u | −6μ_d |
| Δ⁺ | 2μ_u + μ_d | −3μ_d |
| Δ⁰ | **μ_u + 2μ_d** | **0** |
| Δ⁻ | 3μ_d | 3μ_d |

- **Spin weights in the proton:** **⟨σ_z(u₁)+σ_z(u₂)⟩ = 4/3** and **⟨σ_z(d)⟩ = −1/3**. The neutron is the mirror image.
- **Ratio:** μ_p/μ_n = (4μ_u − μ_d)/(4μ_d − μ_u), which is **−3/2** at μ_u = −2μ_d.

**Verdict: CONFIRMED on this leg; both P4 conditions hold.**
- The 4 is the spin-½ weight 4/3.
- The spin-3/2 udd state gives 0, not −3/2, at the same quark-moment ratio.

That meets the P4 condition for DOWNGRADE, which still needs the second leg to agree.
