## The sharp question (what this screen tests)

Route 1 forced the spin-3/2 quartet **given octahedral (2O) symmetry** — 2O has the unique
4-dim genuine irrep. The binary **tetrahedral** group 2T = SL(2,3) has genuine irreps
{2,2,2} — **no 4-dim irrep.** So the μ_n factor-of-4 requires the baryon to be **octahedral,
not merely tetrahedral.** The canonical Borromean representative used throughout the §3.4-G2
thread (three mutually perpendicular golden ellipses, semi-axes φ, 1/φ; = t12067 = the
Borromean rings) has ellipse eccentricity that may break the C₄ octahedral symmetry.

**Question:** Is the rotational symmetry group of the canonical three-golden-ellipse Borromean
config **octahedral O (order 24 → 2O, supports spin-3/2)** or **tetrahedral T = A₄ (order 12
→ 2T = SL(2,3), no spin-3/2 quartet)** or lower?
