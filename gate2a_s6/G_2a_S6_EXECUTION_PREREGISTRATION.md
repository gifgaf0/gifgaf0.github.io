## 0. The question

The distinct-S₄ flag's remaining leg is not a binary identification question like S5's; it is a **grading** question: which part of the spatial reading is import-free, which part requires a geometric import, and where exactly the import sits.

## 1. Eddington guard (the tautology trap)
V4.53 CONSTRUCTED the motion group as the det = +1 signed-permutation matrix group — which is verbatim the octahedral rotation group O ⊂ SO(3) as a matrix group. **"The image is O" is therefore tautological-by-construction and carries no content.** The registered content is: (i) the derivation of that representation from geometric peripheral data (not bookkeeping convention); (ii) properness (det = +1) as a theorem — the parity law; (iii) the rigid/non-rigid dichotomy and the realizability ceilings; (iv) uniqueness class of the spatial identification. No numeric targets exist; the factor-of-4 and μ_n remain sealed.
