## Cluster 1 — Moving impurity in a superfluid: Landau/Cherenkov class (→ D4)

- Mean-field GP: a pointlike or finite (Gaussian) impurity coupled to the condensate density moves drag-free below v_c = c_s and experiences drag above it via Bogoliubov (acoustic) emission — the Landau criterion realized for density-coupled movers. (Astrakharchik–Pitaevskii "Motion of a heavy impurity through a BEC" class; Gaussian-impurity extensions; dGPE force analyses.)
- Beyond mean field: a **sub-critical drag** exists from **Doppler-asymmetric scattering** of quantum fluctuations off a moving impurity — asymmetric Doppler shift of scattered waves ⇒ net force at v < v_c. (Roberts–Pomeau-class proposals; truncated-Wigner confirmations.)
- **Bearing:** (i) confirms the [static-core-direct] class Cherenkov theorem (D4b-i) is standard, settled physics; (ii) the Doppler-asymmetric-scattering drag mechanism has direct prior art — the drive-mediated class (D4b-ii) must have its analogous scattering-drag channel *recorded*, not discovered post hoc. Falls under the locked "sideband kinematics check executed and recorded" clause.

## Cluster 2 — Vortex–sound coupling and multipole order (→ D3, D4a)

- A **co-rotating vortex pair radiates rotating quadrupole sound** (Mitchell–Lele–Moin, JFM 285 (1995); Powell/Möhring acoustic-analogy lineage) — the exact acoustic precedent for orbital-binary emission starting at quadrupole.
- Sound emission by **accelerating** vortex motion and by reconnections is the T→0 dissipation channel in GP superfluids (Leadbeater et al.; Barenghi-school GP simulations); uniform motion is silent. Vortex–density-channel coupling is experimentally direct (vortex-collider sound pulses, Kwon et al., Nature 600, 64 (2021)).
- **Bearing:** supports D3 (quadrupole onset has an exact fluid-dynamical twin) and D4a (acceleration, not uniform motion, radiates).

## Cluster 3 — No supersonic GP traveling defects (→ D4b-i hard anchor)

- **Gravejat, Comm. Math. Phys. 243, 93–103 (2003):** non-existence of non-constant finite-energy traveling waves with speed above the sound speed in GP, dimension N ≥ 2; sonic case: Gravejat, Diff. Int. Eq. 17, 1213–1232 (2004).
- **Jones–Roberts, J. Phys. A 15, 2599 (1982):** the GP traveling-wave family (vortex rings ↔ rarefaction pulses) lives strictly below c_s; upper-branch velocity → c_s only asymptotically. Pitaevskii: multiphonon decay for the portion above the sound line.
- **Bearing:** a GP-native localized defect cannot coherently travel supersonically; combined with Cluster 1, the [static-core-direct] class is structurally KC3-dead at v > c_s. This is the rigorous anchor for the pre-named sharpest risk.

## Cluster 4 — Common-drive response and scattering-mediated interaction (→ D1, D4b-ii)

- Classical secondary-Bjerknes theory: bubbles **driven by a common field** oscillate linearly *at the driving frequency* (classical assumption set) and **scatter** a secondary field; the interbubble force is the time-averaged interaction mediated by that scattered field (Bjerknes 1906; Zabolotskaya; Doinikov–Zavtrak multiple scattering; modern standing-wave experiments). Sign set by drive frequency relative to the two resonances — the CM-3 sign rule's source, unchanged.
- **Bearing:** direct precedent for the D1 structure — response-to-common-drive with scattering-mediated interaction and no independent sourcing; the "oscillates at the drive frequency" steady state is the classical assumption the D1 theorem derives (via radiation-reaction decay of self-modes).
