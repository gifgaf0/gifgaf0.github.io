PHASE 2 — EXPLORE (exploration mode)
Starting requirement: a medium-based vacuum must give matter and light one
shared light cone, and every mode matter couples to must be at least that fast.
Two leads from the review:
  (a) Equation of state. A vacuum with no preferred frame must have
      T_μν ∝ g_μν, so pressure = −energy density: pure tension. Any medium with
      ρ + p ≠ 0 has a rest frame. That's §2.90's "vacuum in perfect tension"
      picture, and the starting point of Klinkhamer–Volovik q-theory.
  (b) Relativistic solitons. In a Lorentz-invariant field theory a moving
      soliton is just a boosted static one: it never radiates, and the shared
      light cone is automatic. Skyrmions (baryons as topological solitons, with
      short-range nuclear binding) and Faddeev–Niemi / Battye–Sutcliffe knots
      (Hopf charge = linking number) are the established home of matter as knots.
