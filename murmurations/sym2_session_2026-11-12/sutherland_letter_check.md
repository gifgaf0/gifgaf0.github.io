# Step 0 follow-up: Sutherland's letter to Rubinstein and Sarnak (read 2026-11-12)

**Source.** A. V. Sutherland, letter to M. Rubinstein and P. Sarnak, dated August 30, 2022, 10 pp. (copy supplied by Matthew; also posted at publications.ias.edu and math.mit.edu/~drew).

**Families plotted** (all ordered by conductor in geometric intervals, x-axis = p up to the lower conductor bound, blue/red = average a_p over even/odd rank):
- isogeny classes of non-CM elliptic curves, N in [2^n, 2^(n+1)), 12 <= n <= 17, and [250000, 500000); also intervals [(3/2)^n, (3/2)^(n+1)), 18 <= n <= 31 (pp. 2, 9-10);
- elliptic curves ordered by naive height, as a contrast: no oscillation (p. 3);
- weight 2, 4, 6 newforms on Gamma_0(N), by Galois-orbit size (pp. 4-6);
- generic genus 2 L-functions, ST group USp(4) (degree 4) (p. 7);
- genus 2 with ST group SU(2)xSU(2) (imprimitive: no oscillation) and N(SU(2)xSU(2)) (Hilbert modular forms: oscillation) (p. 8).

**Not in the letter:** symmetric squares, Sym^2 of modular forms or elliptic curves, any degree-3 family, any (p/N)^(1/3) scaling. So the letter does not cover the level-1 Sym^2 family, and Step 0's conclusion stands. The source of Kundu–Müller's remark about degree-3 regularity in (p/N)^(1/3) is not this letter, and is still unidentified.

**Relevant points, for context only:**
- Sutherland conjectures the phenomenon holds for any family of primitive arithmetic L-functions ordered by conductor (p. 1). Sym^2 f is primitive (cuspidal on GL(3), Gelbart–Jacquet), so this is consistent with the session's choice of the conductor window; it is a heuristic remark, not evidence.
- The two mirrored curves in every plot are the even/odd rank split. Their spread widens with p because a_p is not normalized by sqrt(p) for elliptic curves. Neither feature can appear in the Sym^2 family at level 1, where every root number is +1.
