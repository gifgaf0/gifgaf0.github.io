# E5 — Add light: independent cross-check

*Independent second computation and audit of `seed/experiments/E5_add_light/` (PLAN.md §4 E5). All code is in `crosscheck/` and was written without reading the original scripts. I read the original code only afterwards, to diagnose the text discrepancies listed below. Reproduce with `crosscheck/run_crosscheck.sh`: 3 min 46 s on one core, with thread variables set to 1.*

## Verdict: **confirmed with corrections** (no blocking or major issues)

I recomputed every headline number with different methods. **57 of 57 numeric rows agree** with `results.json`, mostly to 1e-9 relative or better. Every preregistered calibration also passes in my computation. The corrections are all minor: wording, a formula typo in the report text, a mis-stated peak, and generous verdict words.

**My own convergence checks.**
- Shooting with X = 25/40/80, x_m = 2.5/1.5/1.0, x₀ = 1e-3/3e-3 and rtol 1e-13/1e-11 gives F′(0) to 1e-9 and C to 7e-8 (X ≥ 40).
- Two grids (X = 400 with 8×10 angles, X = 200 with 6×8 angles) agree to ≤ 3e-8 in every observable.
- Virial (E₂ − E₄)/M = 1e-13, and B = 1 − 1e-14.
- The axial sphere integral converges with the cutoff: 18.0926 at R = 50 and 18.09259580 at R = 200.

**π⁰ derivation.** At linear order T_β = i ∂_βπ⁰/f, i.e. 3 Tr(Q²τ₃) = 1. This gives L = [N_c e²/(48π²f)] ε^{μναβ} F_μν A_α ∂_βπ⁰ = [N_c e²/(24π²f)] ε ∂_μA_ν A_α ∂_βπ⁰. The amplitude is A = N_c e²/(12π²f), and Γ/[α²m³N_c²/(576π³f²)] = 1 exactly. With anomaly-consistent charges, N_c Tr(Q²τ₃) = 1 (reproduced).

## Issues found (all minor)

1. **π⁰ coupling typo in REPORT Method.** The report writes "(N_c e²/48π²f) ε^{μναβ} ∂_μA_ν A_α ∂_βπ⁰". Written with ∂_μA_ν, that coefficient is a factor 2 too small and contradicts the quoted amplitude N_c e²/(12π²f). The code uses (∂_μA_ν − ∂_νA_μ) = F_μν and is correct. **Fix:** write F_μν, or use 24π². Numbers are unaffected.
2. **Convergence wording.** The report says "X = 25, 50, 100, 200, 400 … agree to within 5×10⁻⁸ in Λ". Its own `hedgehog_log.txt` shows X = 25 off by 8e-7 in Λ and 1.2e-6 in C, with B = 0.99999944. The 5e-8 spread holds for X = 100–400 only.
3. **Neutron enclosed charge.** "Peaks at +0.185 at 1 fm" is a table grid point. The true maximum is **+0.206 at R = 0.73 fm** (massless ANW); for massive AN84 it is +0.140 at 0.68 fm (`cc_extra.json`).
4. **"38/38" overstates what was computed.** Check 10d (no quadratic WZ term) is hard-coded True "by inspection", and checks 5c and 7b pass only up to an overall sign. The statements are true, and the cross-check confirms them.
5. **Verdict vocabulary.** CALIBRATED is used for internal checks with no literature target (symbolic derivations, integer charges). QUANTIFIED is applied to a SEED 6B claim, but PLAN reserves it for §8 gaps. "One parameter set covers all predictions (anomaly sector included)" is the agent's paraphrase, not SEED.md's text.
6. **SEED 6B framing.** "3 of 7 within 30%" treats μ_p (−33%), μ_n (−31%) and g_πNN (−32%) as failures of "roughly 30%". The AMENDED verdict is justified by g_A (−52%) and by the infinite r_p and ⟨r²⟩_n in the massless model. Suggested wording: "6 of 7 finite quantities are within about 33%; the exceptions are g_A and the individual charge radii, which need a pion mass."
7. **Commitment 4, CONFIRMED.** The preregistered prediction holds: the isovector ⟨r²⟩ diverges linearly, as 0.411 fm × R, and becomes finite with m_π. But any extended charge distribution has an R-dependent enclosed charge, so the test cannot tell "dressing" from ordinary structure, and "not slowed" holds by construction. The report states this caveat. The verdict should say "in the preregistered operational sense".
8. **g_πNN calibration.** It passes only under the post hoc ±0.05 tolerance, which is disclosed; at ±0.02 it would fail (|Δ| = 0.032). This is acceptable because the tolerance is rounding-based and g_A, which fixes g_πNN through Goldberger–Treiman, passes its preregistered tolerance.
9. **N_c = 1 exclusion.** It holds only with fixed quark charges, and the report's own extra shows those charges give fractional baryon charges at N_c = 1. The qualifier belongs in the verdict line, not only in the text.

**Checked and found correct:**
- **Citations**, each read in the arXiv mirror text:
  - Bernstein–Holstein eqs. 3, 67–71. Their eq. 69 second line is indeed a factor 2 off for F̃ = ½εF, as the original notes.
  - Ma–Harada eqs. 4.116 and 4.121. Their normalisation is twice ours, as the original states.
  - hep-ph/0605305 eq. 24: ρ_M ∝ r²F′sin²F, which is the ANW magnetic-radius definition.
  - Bär–Wiese hep-ph/0105258 could not be fetched from the mirror; it is consistent with my knowledge.
- **Inputs:** PDG 2026 values and the PrimEx-II value.
- **Figures:** `fig_charge_densities.png` and `fig_vs_pdg.png` exist, are under 300 KB and show the stated content.
- **Text vs results.json:** every number in the report text matches results.json, except item 3. "D = −2C to 3e-10" is the relative form of results.json's absolute 5.4e-9.
- **Small refit difference:** the 7e-6 difference in the SU(3) refit comes from the original averaging the unrounded PDG masses, where I used 1151.19 and 1382.11.
