# Step 1: which field spaces give a three-valued label carried by three strands
Exploration note, October 30, 2026. Citations are tagged [memory] where they have not been checked against the source; verify them before any claim.

## Three routes to "thirds"
| Route | Where the 1/3 comes from | Is 1/3 exact? | Prior art |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Three U(1) phases, one gauge field** (abelian, T³ vacuum manifold) | A vortex in component k carries flux ρ_k/Σρ of a quantum | **Only if** the three densities are equal. That needs a symmetry permuting the components (S₃ or ℤ₃); otherwise the fractions are arbitrary real numbers | Babaev, PRL 89, 067001 (2002) [memory]; Goryo–Soma–Matsukawa, EPL (2007) [memory] |
| **B. U(3) colour-flavour-locked vortices** (non-abelian) | A winding diag(e^{iθ}, 1, 1) carries 1/3 of the U(1) flux because of the ℤ₃ centre | **Yes, exactly**, by group structure | Hanany–Tong (2003); Auzzi, Bolognesi, Evslin, Konishi, Yung (2003) [memory] |
| **C. PSU(3) = SU(3)/ℤ₃ order parameter** | π₁(PSU(3)) = ℤ₃: a three-valued topological charge | **Yes**, topologically | 't Hooft centre vortices (1978) [memory] |

**Ruled out as sources of thirds:**
- **S² (Faddeev–Hopf) and SU(2) (Skyrme).** π₁ is trivial for both, and their labels are integers.
- **SU(3) itself.** π₁ is trivial.

## How this connects to earlier work
- **Route A needs a symmetry permuting three components.** The ℤ₃ and S₃ found today in the box-kite and torus work are candidates for that role. The torus reading gives ℤ₃ cycling three lines, which would force equal densities, so it is a possible link and nothing more yet.
- **Routes B and C put the thirds into the group** and need no extra symmetry.

## The caveat that matters
- **These are thirds of magnetic flux, not of electric charge.** Turning flux into electric charge needs a duality step, the "dual superconductor" picture of confinement (Mandelstam; 't Hooft). That step has not been taken here.
- **The analogy with quarks** is structural: fractional objects that cannot be isolated.

## Recommendation
- **Route A for computing.** It is the simplest, and it was tested in Step 2.
- **Route B as the target.** It gives exact thirds without assuming equal densities.
